01 — Running as a Lifestyle

Running Participation Over Time

How has national running participation evolved across survey years?

Running participation in Australia has increased from under 3 million in 2015 to over 4 million. It rose sharply during COVID-19 in 2020 as more people took up outdoor exercise, and has continued to grow since then.

Running vs Other Activities

How does running compare to alternative fitness activities?

Running is one of Australia's popular fitness activities, behind walking and gym workouts. Despite many other options, running remains a popular choice for Australians.

02 — Who Runs?

Running Frequency Distribution

How frequently do different age cohorts engage in running?

Running frequency differs by age group. Older people tend to run more regularly each week, while younger people balance running with other sports. Experienced runners often run more frequently.

Participant Gender Composition

What is the gender balance among active runners?

Australian runners include both men and women in fairly balanced numbers. Although participation varies by age and race distance, running remains popular among both genders.

Participation by Age Group

Which age demographics record the highest runner engagement?

Running is popular across different age groups, with participation highest among working-age adults. Understanding these age patterns helps plan suitable community programs and running events.

03 — Where Do People Run?

Running Participation Rates by State

How do state participation rates scale relative to population?

Running participation varies across Australian states. Victoria and Western Australia have higher participation rates compared to their populations, showing that running is a popular part of local lifestyles.

Total Runner Population Volume

Where are absolute runner volumes concentrated nationally?

New South Wales and Victoria have the most runners due to their large populations. Smaller territories may have higher participation rates relative to their populations, but contribute fewer runners overall.

Geographic Distribution of Running Events

How are race-day events geographically dispersed across Australia?

Running events are mainly located in major cities and coastal areas across Australia. This gives runners access to events of different distances in many communities.

04 — From 5K to Marathon

Participation by Distance

Which standard race categories attract the most participants?

Shorter races like 5K and 10K attract the most participants, making them popular choices for beginners and casual runners.

Distance Popularity Ranking

How have distance category popularity rankings shifted over time?

Running distance preferences have changed over the years, showing how runners' interests and event trends evolve over time.

Completion-Time Distribution

How do finish times vary across different race distances?

Finish times increase with race distance. Marathons also show a wider range of finish times because runners have different endurance levels and abilities.
